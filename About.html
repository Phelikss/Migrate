About Migrate

A simpler way to find a place near campus.

Migrate helps students of Presbyterian University of East Africa find available rentals around Thogoto and Kikuyu. Browse listings, compare rent and property details, and contact landlords directly.

How it works

  • Students can browse houses and save favourites to revisit later.
  • Landlords and agents can post listings with photos and contact details.
  • New listings are reviewed before they appear publicly.

Stay safe when renting

Listing review is not a guarantee: Migrate does not independently verify landlords or properties and does not handle rent payments. Visit a property in person and confirm the details before paying anything.
